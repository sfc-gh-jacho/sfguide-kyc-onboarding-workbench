# Bundled fonts

- **Inter** by Rasmus Andersson: https://github.com/rsms/inter
- **JetBrains Mono** by JetBrains: https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
They are bundled because Streamlit in Snowflake can't load fonts from remote URLs.
